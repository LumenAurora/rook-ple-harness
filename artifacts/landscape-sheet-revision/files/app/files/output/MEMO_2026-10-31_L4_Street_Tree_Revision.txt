2. What comment L-4 requires
- The comment: CCPW first-round comment L-4 (binding, letter dated
  10/09/2026): street frontage planting at one tree per 20 linear feet per
  Title 30 ch. 30.64 (sec. 30.64.030(k), medium-canopy tier per Guidance
  Memo CP-PW-GM-2025-04). Based on 4,160 lf of Phase 3 street frontage,
  208 trees are required against 58 shown - a shortfall of 150. The comment
  requires the revised planting plan and a revised annual irrigation demand
  with the resubmittal.
- Tree allocation: per the L-2 streetscape frontage summary - Windmill Ln.
  north frontage 2,560 lf: 128 trees (34 shown); Phase 3 interior
  collector, both sides 1,180 lf: 59 trees (16 shown); Rainbow Blvd. entry
  frontage 420 lf: 21 trees (8 shown). Total 208.
- Irrigation demand: TM Condition 28 requires the irrigation demand
  worksheet to be updated for any revision to the planting. At the current
  per-tree allowance (2,000 gal/tree/yr), street tree demand rises from
  116,000 to 416,000 gal/yr; total Phase 3 tree demand from 148,000 to
  448,000 gal/yr (74 to 224 trees including entry and park interior); total
  annual irrigation demand from 3.28 to approximately 4.21 acre-ft/yr
  (+0.92 acre-ft/yr). The revised worksheet goes in the resubmittal
  package under the same cover.
- Canopy target: staff also apply a 30% 15-year shade-coverage target
  (CP-PW-GM-2025-04). Adding the 150 trees in the approved palette should
  satisfy or improve the ratio; Ocotillo to verify against the revised
  plan by 11/13. If any frontage still falls short, the guidance calls for
  additional trees or higher-canopy substitutions - which would raise the
  count above 208 - and we would flag it to you immediately rather than
  let it surface at second-round review.
